# weekly-review

A once-a-week point on your projects and roles. Short, honest, actionable.

## Steps
1. **Read the ground truth.** List active projects (`~/Vault/1-Projects/*.md` with `status: active`)
   and open ideas (`3-Garden/ideas` with `status: seed`).
2. **For each active project**, one line: what moved this week, what is blocked, the single next step.
3. **Spot the drift.** Anything `active` that has not moved in two weeks? Flag it: keep, pause, or
   drop. Update its `status` honestly.
4. **Pick next week's one bet.** The single thing that matters most. Name its kill condition (what
   would tell you to stop).
5. **Write the note** in `3-Garden/` (or `0-Home/` if you keep a running log), dated, `type: log`.
6. Keep it under a page. A review you dread is a review you skip.

## Notes
- Be concrete. "Advancing well" hides the real risk; "573 commits but zero real users" names it.
- The point is to decide, not to describe.
