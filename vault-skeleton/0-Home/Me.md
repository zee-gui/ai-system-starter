# Me

The anchor note. An AI that reads this should know who you are, what you want, and how you work.
Fill it in your own words. Keep it true and short. Update it when your reality changes.

## Who I am
One paragraph: your role, your background, what you are known for.

## What I want (now)
The goal that orders your priorities right now. Be concrete.

## How I work
- What you are good at.
- What you avoid or delegate.
- Standards you hold (quality, tone, non-negotiables).

## How I want AI to work with me
- The voice and format you expect.
- What to always do, what to never do.
- Where to find context before answering (this vault, which project).

## Roles I hold (`caps`)
List your four to six roles. These become the `caps` vocabulary in your notes. See
`4-Tools/frontmatter-spec.md`.
