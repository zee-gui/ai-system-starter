You are helping me set up my personal AI system for the first time. Interview me, one short question
at a time, to fill in my context. Do not dump a wall of questions; ask, wait, then ask the next.

Cover, in order:
1. Who I am (role, background, what I am known for). Write the answer into `~/Vault/0-Home/Me.md`
   under "Who I am".
2. What I want right now (the goal that orders my priorities). Write it under "What I want (now)".
3. My active projects. For each, create `~/Vault/1-Projects/<name>.md` from the project template.
4. My roles (the four to six hats I wear). Write them under "Roles I hold" in `Me.md` and use them
   as my `caps` vocabulary.
5. One recurring task I would like to turn into a skill later. Note it in `0-Home/Me.md`.

Follow `~/Vault/4-Tools/conventions.md` and `frontmatter-spec.md` for every note you write. Keep my
words, do not embellish. When done, show me the file tree you created and commit the vault.
